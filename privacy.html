OneBeam Smart TV Remote — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · App: OneBeam Smart TV Remote (com.onebeam.remote) · Developer: Halo Apps (Akilesh Reddy Katipelly), Hyderabad, India

OneBeam Remote turns your phone into a remote control for smart TVs on your home Wi-Fi. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What stays on your phone

The TVs you add (name, brand, IP address on your local network) and the pairing keys your TVs give the app are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Your TVs and your home network

To find and control TVs, the app scans your local Wi-Fi network and talks directly to your TVs. These messages stay on your home network. We do not run servers that receive this information.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and process the following to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, app version),
  • ad interactions and diagnostics (for example, ad views, clicks and crashes in the ad SDK).

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads load, using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time under OneBeam Pro → Ad choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (OneBeam Pro)

OneBeam Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the app only receives confirmation of what you own so it can turn off ads. We never see your card or payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
